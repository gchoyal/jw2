A Legacy Born in 1955

"Gold is more than an ornament. In our families, it is a covenant of blessings, love, and sacred memory."

Founded in 1955 (स्था. १९५५) in the heart of Madhya Pradesh, Choksiwala Jewellers began as a devoted atelier of master goldsmiths. For over seventy continuous years, we have preserved the sacred art of handcrafting gold jewellery, carrying traditional Malwa motifs alongside modern, refined silhouettes.

Unlike modern trading houses, we are true manufacturers and retailers. Every piece begins with certified 24-karat gold, alloyed precisely to the 22-karat (916) benchmark, shaped, filigreed, and hand-polished in our state-of-the-art workshop. This eliminates middleman overheads and delivers direct workshop value to every bride and family.

Today, with flagship architectural facilities in Kukshi and Barwani, we remain dedicated to the very principles of honesty, hallmark transparency, and personal family warmth that started our journey in 1955.
